## Contact

### Security issues

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately to **sirmarkz@**. Do not open a public issue for security reports.

### Partnership and integration inquiries

Composability with other skill packs, marketplace listings, integration PRs into adjacent projects, joint write-ups: **sirmarkz@**.

### Everything else

Open a GitHub issue or discussion. Do not DM maintainers for general support.

## How To Become A Maintainer

Repeated, high-quality contributions that pass [STYLE.md](STYLE.md) and the validation suite. Maintainers are added by consensus of existing maintainers, not by request.
